About Us

Story of how Camilo Bar + Cafe began

Camilo Bar + Cafe began with a simple idea: to create a place where people could enjoy good coffee, delicious food, and meaningful moments together. Located in the heart of Santa Barbara, Iloilo, the café was imagined as a cozy space where friends could catch up, families could spend time together, and anyone could take a break from their busy day. The idea for Camilo started when its founders noticed how cafés were becoming more than just places to drink coffee. They were becoming spaces where people could meet, share stories, work, celebrate special moments, and simply enjoy each other's company. Inspired by the warm and welcoming culture of the Ilonggo community, they wanted to build a café that felt comfortable and familiar almost like a second home.

More About Us

What started as a small dream gradually became Camilo Bar + Cafe. The name “Camilo” was chosen to represent a place that is friendly, memorable, and easy to call home. From carefully prepared coffee to satisfying meals and sweet treats, every part of the café was created with the goal of giving customers a relaxing and enjoyable experience. As the café grew, Camilo became more than just a place for coffee. It became a gathering place for the local community. Students could spend time studying, friends could meet after a long day, families could enjoy a meal together, and visitors could experience a small taste of the warmth of Santa Barbara. Today, Camilo Bar + Cafe continues to represent the simple things that bring people together: good food, great coffee, warm conversations, and unforgettable moments. Its story is built not only by the people who created it, but also by every customer who walks through its doors and becomes part of the Camilo experience.

Mission, Vision & Values

Mission

To provide quality coffee, delicious food, and a warm, welcoming atmosphere where people can relax, connect, and create meaningful moments. We aim to give every customer a satisfying experience through friendly service, fresh products, and genuine Filipino hospitality.

Vision

To become a beloved local café in Santa Barbara, Iloilo, known for great coffee, delicious food, and a welcoming community. We envision Camilo Bar + Cafe as a place where people can always feel at home, whether they are meeting friends, spending time with family, or simply enjoying a quiet moment.
